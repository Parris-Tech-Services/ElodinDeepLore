Design Use

This project is meant to feed future Rothfuss-game design. It keeps dangerous ground visible: what is textually true, what is author-commentary, and what is still speculative.

Character Focus

Master Namer. Former Chancellor. Haven escapee. Controlled chaos.

Elodin is one of the most destabilizing figures in the Chronicle because he feels unserious on the surface and deeply dangerous underneath. The goal here is to build the fullest possible picture without flattening the mystery that makes him work.

Build the fullest picture possible, but never explain him so neatly that he stops feeling dangerous.

Research rule

Canon first. Meta second. Theory third. Never blur the boundaries.
